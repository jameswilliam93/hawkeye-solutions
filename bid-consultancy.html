The service explained

What is bid consultancy & review?

Bid consultancy and review is the expert layer between your team's draft and a winning submission. You have the knowledge of your business, your services and your track record. What you may not have is the specialist understanding of how procurement teams score bids, what evaluators are looking for between the lines, and how to structure a response that scores maximum marks.

That gap is where bids are lost. Not because the business is not capable of winning the contract - but because the written response does not make the case clearly enough, does not address the scoring criteria precisely, or leaves gaps that a competitor's more polished submission does not.

Our bid consultancy and review service closes that gap. We take your draft, assess it against the scoring criteria, identify exactly where marks are being left on the table, and return a submission-ready response that genuinely reflects the strength of your business.

It is the most cost-effective way to access professional bid expertise - and increasingly the way that forward-thinking recruitment agencies and IT consultancies are building competitive tendering capability without the overhead of a full bid writing engagement every time.

What we do

What our bid review service covers

A structured, expert review that goes far beyond a proofread. We interrogate your bid the way a procurement evaluator would - then fix what we find.

Scoring Criteria Analysis

We map every response against the stated scoring criteria and mark scheme. Where your draft is not directly answering the question the evaluator is scoring, we identify it, explain what is missing and rewrite to address the gap. Many bids fail not because the answer is wrong, but because it does not land against the specific criteria.

Win Theme Development

A strong bid does not just answer questions - it builds a consistent narrative about why your business is the right choice. We identify the win themes your draft is missing, strengthen the ones that are there, and ensure that your unique strengths come through in every response rather than being buried in generic statements.

Quality and Clarity Rewrite

We rewrite sections where the language is unclear, the structure is hard to follow or the response undersells your capability. This is not light editing - it is a professional rewrite that preserves your content and evidence but packages it the way an evaluator wants to read it. The evidence stays yours; the delivery becomes ours.

Compliance and Submission Check

Before anything goes back to you for final review, we check the full submission for compliance: word counts, mandatory attachments, portal requirements and format specifications. Missing a compliance requirement costs marks regardless of how strong the written content is. We make sure nothing slips through.

Why a good business still needs a great bid

Evaluators score what they see, not what you mean

A procurement evaluator will not infer capability from vague statements or give credit for effort. They score against a mark scheme, and if your response does not explicitly address the criteria, the marks do not come. The process

How our bid consultancy & review works

A clear, four-step process from draft submission to a polished, submission-ready bid.

1

You Share Your Draft

Send us your draft responses along with the full tender documents, scoring criteria and any evaluator guidance. The more context you provide, the sharper our review.

2

We Assess Against Criteria

Our bid consultants read your draft the way an evaluator would, mapping every response against the scoring criteria and identifying exactly where marks are being lost and why.

3

We Rewrite and Strengthen

We rewrite weak sections, sharpen win themes, improve structure and close compliance gaps. You receive a tracked-changes document so you can see precisely what changed and why.

4

Final Review and Sign-Off

You review the revised submission, request any final adjustments and confirm sign-off. We do a last compliance check before you submit. Turnaround times are agreed upfront and always met.
