The case for training

Why invest in bid writing training?

Most businesses that lose bids do not lose because their service is inferior. They lose because the people writing the bid have never been taught how procurement teams actually score responses - and without that understanding, even a strong business will consistently underperform on paper.

Bid writing is a specific, learnable skill. It requires an understanding of how to interpret scoring criteria, how to structure a response that evaluators can mark quickly and clearly, how to build win themes that differentiate your business, and how to evidence capability in a way that genuinely shifts scores. None of this is intuitive, and very little of it is taught in standard business or marketing training.

Our bid writing training fills that gap. Delivered by practitioners who write and review live bids every day, it is grounded in what actually works across recruitment sector frameworks, IT consultancy tenders and public sector procurement - not generic advice that could apply to any industry.

The goal is not to make your team dependent on a trainer. It is to build the internal capability that lets you compete confidently, consistently and cost-effectively - with our ongoing bid consultancy and review service available as the expert safety net when you need it.

Training content

What our bid writing training covers

Every training programme is tailored to your team and your sector. These are the core modules we build from - delivered in the combination that fits your needs.

Understanding How Bids Are Scored

The foundation of every effective bid response is understanding how evaluators actually mark submissions. We walk through how scoring criteria and mark schemes work in practice, what evaluators are trained to look for, how common scoring models operate (quality weighting, pass-fail gates, price-quality splits) and the most common reasons responses lose marks they should have won.

Reading and Interpreting Tender Documents

ITTs, PQQs, framework applications and Digital Marketplace opportunities all have distinct structures and conventions. We teach your team how to read the full tender pack - not just the questions - to identify what the buyer genuinely values, where the marks are concentrated, and how to spot the strategic signals that separate high-scoring responses from average ones.

Win Themes and Differentiator Development

Generic responses score average marks. We train your team to identify the specific win themes that make your business the right choice for a particular buyer, and to carry those themes consistently through every response. This module covers how to articulate differentiators clearly, how to avoid the vague claims that evaluators disregard, and how to make your bid memorable in a competitive field.

Writing Responses That Score Maximum Marks

The practical writing module covers structure, language, evidence and formatting. We work through real tender questions with your team, showing how to plan a response before writing it, how to use the STAR method and similar frameworks to structure evidence, how to match language to the buyer's own criteria, and how to write clearly and concisely under word count pressure.

Bid Management and Process

A well-written bid submitted late or with a missing attachment loses marks it should not. We cover the practical process of managing a bid from opportunity identification through to submission - including how to set up a bid timeline, assign responsibilities, gather evidence from stakeholders without creating bottlenecks, and run a pre-submission compliance check that catches problems before they cost you.

Sector-Specific Frameworks and Requirements

Generic bid training does not prepare you for the specifics of recruitment sector tendering or IT framework applications. Our sector modules cover the requirements that matter for your market - social value in public sector recruitment bids, TUPE and KPI structures, DOS 7 and G-Cloud application conventions, Crown Commercial Service framework rules and the language that procurement teams in your sector actually use to evaluate suppliers.

What you get from it

What good bid training actually delivers

Training is only worth the investment if it changes how your team performs in real competitions. Here is what our clients see when they put the training to work.

01

Higher scores on quality sections

The quality section of most tenders is where bids are won and lost. Teams that understand how evaluators score responses write answers that are structured to score maximum marks rather than answers that simply cover the ground. The difference is measurable and consistent across multiple submissions.

02

Faster, more confident bid production

Bid writing is stressful when your team is not sure if what they are producing is good enough. Training builds the confidence that comes from understanding what good looks like - and speeds up the production process because your team spends less time second-guessing and more time writing to a clear standard.

03

Reduced reliance on external writers for every bid

Full bid writing is the right choice for major, high-value opportunities. But not every tender warrants that level of investment. Training your team means you can handle the volume of smaller opportunities in-house, reserving external support for the bids where the stakes are highest - and using our bid consultancy and review service as the quality assurance layer when you need it.

By the end of training

What your team will be able to do

Practical outcomes, not theoretical knowledge. Every module is built around what your team needs to do differently when they next sit down to write a bid.

Read a tender pack and identify where the marks are

Rather than working through questions sequentially, your team will know how to read a full tender pack strategically - identifying the highest-weighted sections, the buyer's stated and unstated priorities, and the compliance requirements that must be met before quality scores even apply.

Structure a response that evaluators can score quickly

Evaluators often read dozens of responses against a tight mark scheme. A response that is hard to follow, buries its key points or does not match the structure of the question costs marks even when the underlying content is strong. Your team will learn to write for the evaluator, not just for the question.

Write social value responses that genuinely score

Social value is now a mandatory element of most public sector tenders and carries significant weighting in recruitment framework evaluations. Generic social value statements score poorly. Your team will learn how to build credible, evidence-backed social value responses that align with buyer priorities and score in the top tier.

Use evidence and case studies effectively

Most businesses have strong evidence but do not know how to deploy it effectively in a bid context. Your team will learn how to extract the right evidence for a specific question, frame it in a way that evaluators recognise as relevant, and present it concisely without losing the detail that drives high scores.

Run a bid process that does not miss deadlines or details

Procedural failures - missed deadlines, incomplete attachments, wrong formatting - cost marks that have nothing to do with the quality of your service. Your team will leave with a practical bid management process that prevents avoidable errors and keeps every submission on track from opportunity identification to final submission.

Know when to use expert support and when to go it alone

Good training is not about making your team feel they can handle everything without help. It is about building the judgement to know which opportunities you can compete on confidently in-house and which ones warrant bringing in external expertise - whether that is full bid writing for a major framework or a bid consultancy and review for an important call-off.
