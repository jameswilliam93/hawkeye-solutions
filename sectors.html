Recruitment & Staffing

Recruitment sector tendering is one of the most competitive landscapes in UK public procurement. We understand the specific requirements buyers impose on staffing suppliers - social value obligations, TUPE, KPI structures, workforce planning and the framework conventions that govern how suppliers are evaluated.

We have written winning bids across temporary and permanent recruitment, managed service contracts, executive search and neutral vendor frameworks - for agencies ranging from boutique specialists to large national suppliers.
