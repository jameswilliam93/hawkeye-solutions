Tender Description

Mosscare St Vincent's Housing Group - or MSV for short, are a social landlord working across the Northwest of England. We own and manage almost 9,000 homes in Greater Manchester, Lancashire and West Yorkshire. Our ethos and values place us right at the heart of communities, working with customers to deliver excellent landlord services and provide customers with a warm, safe home. MSV invites organisations to submit a request to participate in order to establish a DPS of contractors to provide a wide range of servicing including but not limited to repairs and maintenance, cyclical servicing, mechanical and electrical services and capital works. Categories are listed within this notice and further detail of minimum requirements for each category can be found within the tender documentation.
