Tender Description

BURNLEY BOROUGH COUNCIL (BBC) wishes to appoint a number of Contractors to deliver a range of construction related Reactive Repairs and Maintenance Works to its property portfolio. The intention is to enter into the Framework agreement in April 2027 for a period of four years. Scope of Services, Framework Lots and Appointments to the Framework The scope of Services to be delivered under the Framework Agreement will be a range of construction works for Reactive Repairs and Maintenance Works and will be divided into the following Lots: - Framework Lots Rotation Lots (General Fabric Works) Lot 1 - General Building Fabric (Low value up to £10k) Lot 2 - Joinery and Internal Finishes Lot 3 - Bespoke Joinery Lot 4 - Plastering, Rendering and Dry Lining Lot 5 - Brickwork, Masonry and External Fabric Lot 6 - Roofing and Weatherproofing (Flat and pitched) Lot 7 - Cladding and Rainscreen Systems Lot 8 - Windows, Doors and Access (General) Lot 9 - Industrial Doors, Shutters and Barriers Lot 10 - G
