Tender Description

Peabody Trust intends to establish a Dynamic Market under the Procurement Act 2023 for the procurement of Works related contracts by Peabody Trust and other eligible contracting authorities. Please head to www.procurepublic.co.uk/tenders to access the application form. ProcurePublic Ltd acts as the procurement and managing agent for the Dynamic Market and will manage its establishment, supplier admission, administration and ongoing operation on behalf of Peabody Trust. The Dynamic Market is intended to provide a flexible and compliant procurement route for a broad range of works associated with housing, property, construction, regeneration, asset management, maintenance, compliance, infrastructure and the wider public sector estate. The Dynamic Market will be divided into individual categories (parts) to enable contracting authorities to identify suppliers appropriate to the nature, location, value and requirements of a particular procurement.
