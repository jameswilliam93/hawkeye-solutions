Tender Description

Peabody Trust intends to establish a Dynamic Market under the Procurement Act 2023 for the procurement of services and associated supplies by Peabody Trust and other eligible contracting authorities. Please head to www.procurepublic.co.uk/tenders to access the application form. ProcurePublic Ltd is appointed by Peabody Trust to act as the procurement and managing agent for the Dynamic Market and will manage its establishment, supplier admission, administration and ongoing operation on behalf of Peabody Trust. The Dynamic Market is intended to provide a flexible and compliant procurement route covering professional, technical, operational, corporate, property, housing and resident-facing services required by contracting authorities. The Dynamic Market will be divided into individual categories to enable contracting authorities to identify suppliers appropriate to the nature, location, value and requirements of a particular procurement.
