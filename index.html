Public sector procurement

Understanding the landscape

Public sector procurement is governed by the Procurement Act 2023 and requires suppliers to respond to structured tender questions across quality, price, and social value. With the right expertise, it is one of the most reliable routes to sustainable contract growth, and Hawkeye Solutions is here to guide you through it.

The people behind your success
Meet the Team
Philip Pitt
Founder and Managing Director

Philip has over 24 years of recruitment experience working across large, small and niche agencies, delivering recruitment services and IT consultancy to the public and private sector including Government, NHS, IT, utilities and financial services. For the last 14 years he has provided specialist bid, proposal and tender writing services across all major frameworks including managed services, temporary and permanent recruitment and executive search. Business wins include multiple frameworks run by Crown Commercial Service, Workforce Alliance, HTE, Local Authorities, utilities, charities and private sector companies.

James Pitt
Senior Director, Business Development

James brings a background in finance and a sharp commercial instinct to Hawkeye Solutions, having transitioned into business development and bid management to help drive the company's growth. Since joining the team James has played a pivotal role in securing significant client relationships, with particular success across G-Cloud and Digital Outcomes and Specialists frameworks. Working alongside his father Philip, James has helped expand Hawkeye's reach across the utilities sector and beyond, building lasting partnerships with organisations navigating the complexities of public sector procurement.
