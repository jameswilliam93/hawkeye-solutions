description: >-
  United Utilities is procuring a Dynamic Market for Management Consultancy
  services. This is to provide a compliant route to market for consultancy-led
  services and consultant resources that support and deliver business
  requirements.


  A Dynamic Market is a procurement route that supports quick and flexible engagement with the Supply Chain. It introduces a list of pre-qualified suppliers, approved to provide a specific range of goods, services, or works. For Management Consultancy, this includes scope across Business Strategy, Change & Transformation, Environmental & Sustainability, HR, Finance, Technical and lots more.


  This provides a compliant route to market in a space where we've historically seen lots of uncontrolled and ad hoc spend. Not only will this create compliance, but it will also help to increase competition, work at pace, and drive value for money – ensuring we spend customers’ money wisely. This Dynamic Market remains open for new suppliers to join at any time, subject to meeting the minimum criteria to be successful, which means greater access to innovation and a broad pool of suppliers.
